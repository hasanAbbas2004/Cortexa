# Cortexa – AI-Powered Business Intelligence Platform
Note: App is in private repository. Just Sharing readme. Deployed on https://cortexa.saynt.org/


## Overview

Cortexa is a production-deployed AI business intelligence platform designed to help startups and small-to-medium businesses manage sales, marketing, and business strategy from a single unified system.

Most businesses rely on multiple disconnected tools for customer outreach, advertising, reporting, and decision-making. This often leads to fragmented data, duplicated work, and limited visibility into overall business performance.

Cortexa addresses this challenge by combining specialized AI agents into a single platform that works collaboratively across departments. Instead of operating as separate tools, these agents share context and business knowledge, enabling more informed recommendations and automated workflows.

The platform is publicly accessible and actively used as a live software system.

---

## Core Components

### Sales Agent

The Sales Agent assists businesses with lead generation, customer engagement, and sales operations.

Key capabilities include:

* Lead discovery and qualification
* Voice and SMS outreach automation
* Customer interaction analysis
* Sales pipeline management
* Follow-up recommendations and tracking

### Marketing Agent

The Marketing Agent helps businesses manage and optimize marketing activities.

Key capabilities include:

* Campaign performance monitoring
* Marketing analytics and reporting
* Return-on-investment (ROI) analysis
* Audience and performance insights
* Data-driven campaign recommendations

### Business Copilot

The Business Copilot serves as the strategic intelligence layer of the platform.

It enables users to interact with their business data through natural language conversations and provides:

* Business performance insights
* Cross-department analysis
* Strategic recommendations
* Historical trend evaluation
* Context-aware decision support

## AI Architecture

At the center of Cortexa is a shared AI Engine that coordinates communication between all platform components.

Rather than operating as isolated AI systems, each agent contributes specialized expertise while sharing relevant business context with the others.

This architecture enables:

* Multi-agent collaboration
* Unified business understanding
* Centralized decision-making logic
* Consistent responses across departments
* Shared organizational knowledge

The AI Engine performs tasks such as:

* Intent classification
* Context management
* Agent orchestration
* Knowledge retrieval
* Large language model integration

---

## Technical Architecture

Cortexa follows a modular monolith architecture.

This approach keeps the entire platform within a single application while maintaining clear separation between business domains such as sales, marketing, and strategic intelligence.

The architecture consists of:

* FastAPI backend services
* MongoDB data storage
* Central AI orchestration layer
* REST-based APIs
* Authentication and security middleware
* Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) capabilities for contextual intelligence

This design allows the platform to scale efficiently while avoiding the operational complexity often associated with microservice-based systems.
